How this works

The attorney is drafting the money documents. This is the rest: the conditions the whole family writes first, in plain language, and then hands over to be put into legal form. It is a starting point, not a finished thing.

Open a section, read it, tap how it sits with you, and add what you would change. Then share your thoughts with the family, or copy the whole draft into the Google Doc so it can be worked on there.

Nothing here sends itself anywhere. What you type stays on this device until you share it. There are no prices, rates, or payments in here; those come after the appraisal. Nothing is final until everyone signs.
