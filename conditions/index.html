How this works

The attorney is writing the loan paperwork. The everyday agreement that goes with it we write ourselves, in plain words, and hand to him to put into legal form.

Open a section, tap how it sits with you, and add what you would change. Then share your thoughts with the family, copy the whole draft into the Google Doc, or download it as a Word file to print and pass around.

What you type stays on your device until you share it. Prices, rates, and payments come after the appraisal. It becomes final when everyone signs.
